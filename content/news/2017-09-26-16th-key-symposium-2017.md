The KeY Symposium brings together researchers interested in KeY
and related aspects. We will exchange recent achievements, current
ideas, discuss the next steps and milestones of the area, as well as
future directions in general. Also the latest developments in the KeY
tool are presented and discussed.

For more information about the 16th KeY Symposium, please follow this
