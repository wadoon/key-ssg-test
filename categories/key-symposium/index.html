Report about the 1st International HacKeYthon 2018

Dec 17, 2018 Dominic Steinhöfel
: KeY Symposium
Table of Contents Results of the Subprojects Interaction logging Proof Management GUI Dialog Streamlining and Extensions for Easier Accessibility Parallelization: Simultaneous Proving of Distinct Branches Algebraic Data Types for KeY Students Award and Social Aspects The First HacKeYthon Will Not Be the Last Two weeks ago, at the 6th and 7th of December, 2018, the 1st International HacKeYthon took place in the rooms of the KIT in Karlsruhe, Germany. In five groups consisting of 3 to 4 participants each we worked on small projects contributing to the overall good of the KeY system.
