Best Paper Award: “Inferring Secrets by Guided Experiments”

Nov 9, 2017 Richard Bubel
: Best Paper
The paper “Inferring Secrets by Guided Experiments” (preprint) from Quoc Huy Do, Richard Bubel and Reiner Hähnle won the Best Paper Award at ICTAC 2017. A program has secure information flow if it does not leak any secret information to publicly observable output. A large number of static and dynamic analyses have been devised to check programs for secure information flow. The authors present an algorithm that can carry out a systematic and efficient attack to automatically extract secrets from an insecure program.
