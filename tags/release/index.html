Release: KeY 2.10

Dec 23, 2021 Wolfram Pfeifer
: Release
Just in time to put it under your christmas tree, we are happy to present the new 2.10 version of KeY. The new version mainly contains improvements under the hood, such as completely rewritten parsers for JML and the internal problem/proof format or better translation of problems to SMT-LIB2. You can download and try it out here. We thank all developers and testers for reaching this milestone! Nice holidays and a happy new year!

Release: KeY 2.8

Dec 18, 2020 Richard Bubel
: Announcement Release
After almost 2 years of active development, we present now KeY 2.8 just before the year’s end. The new KeY version comes with significant improvements on the calculus side, but features also a major overhaul of the user interface.
